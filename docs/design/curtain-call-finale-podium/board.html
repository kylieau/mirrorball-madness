Curtain Call in the Finale: call the podium

Mocks, not production UI. Couples are real; who reaches the Finale, where they finish, the league and the managers are made up. Week 11 only: every other week keeps Who goes home? Points are the default settings with five finalists (3.23 a place, 2.16 for the top scorer), before the league's weight.

1 · Picking

Who goes home? becomes three picks: Winner, Runner-Up and Third Place. A couple can fill only one of them. Top scorer is unchanged. Save needs all three places or none, the way a double elimination needs both.
