2 · The pages the card opens

One shared frame for all three: a back link that says where it goes, a switch between the moves this league has on (gold dot = needs you), the two facts that matter as a strip, then your own to-do first. The rules and the reference lists fold away at the bottom instead of filling the page.

4 · C, with or without the status word

Kylie picked C and asked whether the switch needs its second line. Both variants, in the same two states.

C1 · Plain tabs, a gold dot Recommended

The tabs are only names, one line tall, the same switch as the pages exactly. A gold dot marks a tab that needs you; a tab with nothing to do has nothing. The lines under the switch carry everything else. You lose the "2 claims" / "Accepted" read on the tabs you are not on, but those were never urgent, and every needs-you is still marked.
