Season wrap: every tab after the Finale

Mocks, not production UI. Real Season 35 data: Ezra & Daniella won, Amber & Pasha were runners-up, Connor & Rylee third, Tatyana & Jan 4th, Maura & Mark 5th; league totals are each league's standings after the Finale was published, and the bracket is Kylie's real one. Today nothing changes when the season ends: the banner says That's a Wrap for 7 days then disappears, league cards keep their "Locked" lines, Picks keeps paging weeks and Transactions says there's no run to come. Dashed outlines mark what's new.
