# Dance Card

The drafted-couples module: its v1/v2/v3 rules, roster points and the Couples Leaderboard.

Dated notes such as "(Kylie, 2026-11-10)" record when that behaviour was settled.

- **Dance Card v3 (flat judges' rate, tapering survival; Kylie 2026-11-10, `docs/design/dance-card-v3/`):** a league's `scoring_settings.dance_card_version` picks its Dance Card rule: v1 (judges × multiplier + survival + placement + Fantasy Bonus), v2 (season curve, `docs/design/dance-card-v2/`: `round_cents(k_N × S^0.8)` of the couple's inputs since the Anchor Week, each week paying the change) or v3: judges at one rate all season (`DANCE_CARD_V3_RATES`, 0.2731 for two-couple rosters, pinned to `solveDanceCardV3Rates(3, 0.5)` so a median roster's season stays 100 per unit of weight), survival `3.00 × √n` in total over the couple's survival weeks from the Anchor Week (a week with a settled outcome and none of `NO_SURVIVAL_OUTCOMES`; in the week a winner is named every finalist survives, the ones placed below third and entered as Eliminated included, and only a withdrawal or bye doesn't: `survivingCoupleIds`, Kylie 2026-11-11, shared with Lifeline; the rates stay as solved), the Fantasy Bonus at face value, no placement. v2 and v3 share one ledger, `danceCardLedger` (`src/lib/dance-card-v2.ts`, integer cents, entries carry `survivalCents` / `bonusCents`; `usesDanceCardLedger` / `danceCardLedgerRule`), and v3 needs every outcome row with its `outcome` (the ledger throws otherwise). Engine: `buildWeeklyScoreRows` needs `danceCardPriorWeeks` for a v2/v3 league; publication rebuilds those leagues' later weeks after a correction (`laterDanceCard`, `PLAN_VERSION = 5`, `commit_episode_publication_with_later_weeks`); a reveal rebuilds the whole week and refuses while a later week has scores; `assert_special_dance_plan` refuses a plan below 4 while a league is v2/v3 and below 5 while one is v3 (`supabase/apply-dance-card-v3.sql`). Readers branch on `danceCardRule`. Score History keeps v1's wording (Kylie: the rule changes the numbers, not the copy): under v3 `Harry: Jdg 30 × 0.273`, then Survival and Bonus lines; under v2 the multiplier is that week's credit over its input. Roster lines stay judges-only and Picks' Survival & Bonuses row shows under v1 and v3; the Couples Leaderboard's Bonuses column carries v3 survival and bonus. League Settings shows a v2 league a Dance Card Scoring · Season Curve row and its explanation (Kylie tried v1's fields and kept the explanation, 2026-11-07), and a v3 league the rate and "3.00, Tapering" survival, read-only. Switching, rebuilding and checking a league: `scripts/dance-card-v2.ts` (`--to v3` default, `--to v2` is the way back, `--rollback` is v1) through `set_dance_card_points` (revision, exact coverage, archive, refused mid-night). A v2/v3 league's Anchor Week can't move once it has scores (trigger). The four S35 leagues went v1 → v2 on 2026-11-07 and v2 → v3 on 2026-11-10 before E10 (commissioners agreed both times; each switch's replaced rows are in `weekly_manager_scores_archive`; `--verify` matched and Curtain Call / Grand Finale were unchanged on all 175 rows). `apply-dance-card-v3.sql` is applied live and verified; `apply-dance-card-v3-default.sql` (new leagues start on v3) is applied live too (column default read back 2026-11-11). `scripts/dance-card-v2.ts --verify` after any correction publish.

- **Roster per-couple points are judges' points only** (`judgePointsThroughWeek`); survival/placement bonuses stay manager-level. **Former couples stay on the card** (Kylie, 2026-11-12, `docs/design/roster-history/plan.md` B1): under the current couples, a dimmed card per couple that left the roster before the paged week (`formerSlotsForWeek`: periods ended at or after the Anchor Week, not held again that week), tagged Traded when the period that followed began by trade, Eliminated when the couple has gone home as far as the viewer has watched, else Dropped (`formerCoupleNote`), with the weeks and `now with Pat` for the holder in the paged week, +0.00 and the total it earned; League at a Glance lists the same under each manager. A couple claimed back is one current card over every period (`judgePointsThroughWeek` sums a couple's periods and rounds once). On Your Fantasy Roster, the header is the viewer's Dance Card total *for the carousel's paged week* (weighted `roster_points` for that week, bonuses included — not season-cumulative, not their all-module standings total; the carousel's last page, and where it opens, is the week still to be scored once the viewer has watched the week before it (Kylie, 2026-11-17: a couple won on waivers or by trade shows as soon as it is theirs), reading "Not scored yet" with each couple's total, no Safe tag on a couple still dancing (Kylie, 2026-11-18, `docs/design/coming-week-roster/` A) and League at a Glance's rosters without points), and a per-week "Survival & Bonuses" row (`weeklyBonusPoints`) under the couple lines is what bridges the judges-only lines to that total. **Each open spot is its own dashed gold card on Your Fantasy Roster** (Kylie, 2026-11-20, `docs/design/roster-lifeline/` C), after the current couples and before the former ones (`LifelineSpotCard`, `RosterLifeline`): the Lifeline with `In place of {couple}` (left off when that couple is still held that week, since its Eliminated card sits just above; a traded-away couple keeps it) and a Lifeline tag, reading `½ their week` before the week is scored and its weighted points once it is; with no pick an Open Spot card (`No Lifeline Yet`, `Pick ›` to the Lifeline page until the lock, then a dimmed `No Lifeline` at +0.00). The coming week reads `loadLifelineWeek`; a scored week reads `lifeline_payouts` over `lifelinePicksState`'s spots for that week. League at a Glance keeps peers' `Lifeline · {couple}` lines. When the league's Dance Card weight isn't 1 the card says so in one line, as Curtain Call and Grand Finale do ("Points include your league’s × 0.5 weight.", Kylie, 2026-11-11).

- **Couples Leaderboard** (Standings, `CouplesLeaderboard`): every cast couple ranked by Total = Judges + Bonuses (survival, podium, per-couple bonus) over its season, ties (and the pre-scoring state, when everyone is 0) alphabetical by celebrity; shown as soon as the draft is complete, with all three number columns in the serif, via `buildCouplesLeaderboard` (`src/lib/couples-leaderboard.ts`, pure, parity-tested against `computeWeeklyScores`) with results loaded by `loadCoupleWeekResults` for spoiler-allowed weeks only. Curtain Call and Grand Finale points are manager-level and never appear here. **One row per couple** (Kylie, 2026-11-17, reversing her 2026-11-12 one-row-per-holder pick, option A2 in `docs/design/roster-history/`): each couple is listed once with its whole season from the Anchor Week, whoever held it (`buildCouplesSeasonLeaderboard`, the what-if an undrafted couple always had), so its numbers no longer split by holder; the owner line is the holder now, with `· since Week 10` when their unbroken run started after Week 1, or, for a couple nobody holds now, the last holder and their weeks (`Kylie · Weeks 1–9`); a departed holder reads **Released**. Holdings are clamped to what the viewer has watched (`clampSlotsToWeek`: a holding starting at week N is public once week N-1 is watched, an end past the cutoff reads as open). The couple page's In Your Leagues still lists every holder with that holder's own points, latest first (`buildCouplesLeaderboard`, one row per holder). An undrafted couple shows "Undrafted", set like every other row (no italics); eliminated couples stay, faded. The Standings headings are "Managers Leaderboard" and "Couples Leaderboard".
