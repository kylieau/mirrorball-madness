# Fan Pages

Home, Results, Picks, Standings and the couple page: what each shows, spoiler gating and the live-night chrome.

Dated notes such as "(Kylie, 2026-11-10)" record when that behaviour was settled.

- **League Picks and Standings are separate routes**, `/leagues/[id]/picks` and `/leagues/[id]/standings`, each loading only its own data on top of `loadLeaguePageBase` (`src/lib/league-page-data.ts`: standings, spoiler cutoff, revealing/draft weeks, header data) inside `LeaguePageShell`. `/leagues/[id]` is only a redirect for old links (`?tab=standings` → Standings, anything else → Picks, other params carried over). League-scoped server actions revalidate `/leagues/${id}` with `"layout"` so both pages refresh. The league switcher (`LeagueSwitcher`, on the pinned title row) opens a popover anchored under it, right-aligned, pinned or not (Kylie, 2026-11-11: not a sheet from the top of the screen; `src/components/ui/popover.tsx`), with whole-row links, a Picks Due pill only when due and the Create/Join buttons in a footer that stays visible while the list scrolls; it gets its other leagues' rank and Picks Due when it opens (`getSwitcherSummaries`, `src/app/leagues/[id]/switcher/actions.ts`, through `loadHomeLeagueData`, so a row matches that league's Home card), not from `loadLeaguePageBase`.

- **Home curtain banner** (`EpisodeBanner`, state in `src/lib/episode-banner.ts`, pure and tested): driven by the banner week's *driver* episode (earliest unpublished one) on East-live `airs_at` + `episodes.duration_minutes` (default 120, set on Schedule). States (chip = show clock, title = league gate, sub = next hinge; copy in `statusCopy`, times viewer-local via `formatAirsAt`, US zones shown as PT/ET): Picks Open / Picks Locked (Curtain Call on only, lock = latest `prediction_lock_at` across the viewer's leagues, so it reads Locked only once none is still open; chip Curtain Up Soon, sub "Live On Air · {day time}") / On Air Now (title 💃Let's Dance🕺, chip Live Now (ET) with a live dot, sub "Picks Locked · Time to Vote", or "Picks Still Open · Time to Vote" while a league's lock falls after the East curtain) / Results Soon (chip Curtain Closed) / Scores Are In (chip That's a Wrap; shown for 48h after the week's results are published, and never within 48h of the next air; with no week marked Finale the last week's holds 7 days, then no banner) / **Season wrap** (Kylie, 2026-11-24: once the week marked Finale is fully published, after the West feed, with no hold until another season is active; title the champion couple, sub `🪩 Mirrorball Champions` and 2nd / 3rd, a trophy ending the track; a viewer who hasn't watched the Finale gets "Season Complete · Mark the Finale watched to see who won"; names from `loadSeasonChampions`, `src/lib/season-wrap-data.ts`, through that viewer's spoiler-clamped statuses). Two global West Coast overlays win over every other state: Hold the Curtain (East end to 8pm PT; chip Spoiler Lockdown, sub "West Coast Showtime · {day time}") and 💃Let's Dance🕺 (8-10pm PT; chip Live Now (PT) with a live dot, sub "No spoilers, darling"). With Curtain Call off the picks phrasing drops (Picks Open/Locked read Curtain Up Soon, On Air Now's sub is just "Time to Vote"). The client re-derives the state (every minute within 6h of any air time, otherwise daily or when that window opens). The season track (`seasonTrack`) has one dot per week through the week marked Finale on Schedule (`competition_weeks.is_finale`), so S35 shows 11; with no Finale marked it pads to seven dots and grows past seven once more weeks are checked. It glows the banner's own week marked **Now** while it is on air or awaiting results and **Next** while it is still to come; on Scores Are In that week is checked and the glow moves to the next week, marked Next. "Airs" text is relative (Today/Tomorrow/weekday), viewer-local and computed after mount.

- **Home Recent Activity** (`buildRecentActivity`, `src/lib/home-activity.ts`, pure and tested): season-global, so one line per eliminated couple and one "{Celebrity} scored N on their {Dance}" line per `dance_scores` row, never repeated per league, plus per-league roster moves at the top of the week whose waiver run made them (`homeTransactions`, `src/lib/home-transactions.ts`: "Kim traded Harry & Witney and 4.50 pts to you for Alix & Val in Alpha", "Kim, Pat and you made a trade in Alpha" for three or more managers, "You added Jo & Ezra and dropped Alix & Val in Alpha"; awarded claims and trades that went through only, read through RLS, none until that SQL is applied) and per-league overtakes ("Kim overtook Pat and you for 2nd in Alpha", after that week's eliminations; `weeklyRankMoves`, `src/lib/league-rank-moves.ts`, recomputed from `weekly_manager_scores` on every load, so a revealing week shows its net movement so far, never each swap; strict, so ties and a 0–0 start add none), in an inner scroller (`h-72`), newest week first, each week top to bottom as roster moves, overtakes, eliminations, then scores (Kylie, 2026-11-10: read upward, scores led to the elimination, which moved the standings), over spoiler-allowed weeks only (`cutoff.visibleEpisodes`). While the West feed is airing (`west_soon`/`west_watching`) that week's eliminations, roster moves and overtakes collapse to "Week N results are in" (render-time only) whenever the week's results are posted, a week where nobody went home included (`ActivityWeek.resultsIn`); posted score lines still show. Scores are newest post first. A week being revealed (`findRevealingWeek`, `src/lib/revealing-week.ts`: an unpublished episode with live `dance_scores`) is included through `scoredWeekIds` — only for viewers (Spoiler-Free on or off) who chose Stay Updated or Mark Watched for that week — in Home activity and league rank/points (and the pages above); `RevealAutoRefresh` re-fetches Home, Results, Picks and Standings every 20s while a visible week is revealing or while the clock is inside an episode night's window (`liveRefreshWindows`: 30 minutes before the East curtain to the end of the West feed, checked on the client so a page opened before the show still switches on). For a Spoiler-Free viewer, Home shows one `SpoilerFreeStrip` in the sticky brand stack (Pattern B, Soft inset — `docs/design/spoiler-free-strip-placement/` and `docs/design/spoiler-free-strip-styles/`: wordmark + avatar, then a mauve strip with one hairline, a gold status dot, and a gold Mark Watched pill; the page title and the strip stay stuck together on scroll and the wordmark + avatar row scrolls away (Kylie, 2026-11-11: the title is the sticky, not the branding, and the avatar isn't pinned); only while there is a week to unlock: results ready, or scores posting now with a pulsing dot; caught-up viewers see nothing and the pinned title row remains; every fan tab pins its title through `FanPageChrome` → `PinnedPageHeader`, `src/components/top-bar.tsx` / `pinned-page-header.tsx`: the wordmark + avatar row in flow, then a sticky block of the `PageHeader` title row with the tab's control right-aligned on it (`trailing`: the league switcher on Picks/Standings, or the one league's name) and the strip under it; Results keeps its full week carousel centred under the gold rule while in flow and swaps it for the compact one on the title row once the block pins (`pinnedTrailing`, an IntersectionObserver on a marker above the block), so the block gets shorter at that point, and Results' content is `min-h-dvh` so that shrink can never unpin it on a short week; the block pins under the phone's top inset, which a fixed cover paints; the couple page keeps `StickyTopBar`, guests keep `SiteHeader`) on one line ("Spoiler-Free · Week N results are in" / "Week N posting live" + Mark Watched, or "Watching live · Week N" with no button while a week is still posting and the viewer's mark already covers it) whose button confirms in a bottom sheet that confirms the latest week (one tap), whose description is always "Scores, dances, and eliminations will show through this week.", with "Choose an Earlier Week" opening a vertical "I've Watched Through…" list only when more than one week is unmarked (marking a week always marks the earlier ones too). **Live-air prompt** (`LiveScoresPrompt`, state from `buildLiveAirChrome` in `src/lib/spoiler-free-strip-state.ts`, windows from `liveAirPhase` in `src/lib/episode-banner.ts`): shown to everyone, Spoiler-Free on or off, on Home, Results, Picks and Standings, only inside the East window (`airs_at` + `duration_minutes`) or the West window (8-10pm PT), never before the curtain, in the ET→PT gap or after the night, and only once that week has a released draft or a live couple. Title "Scores Are Going Live", chip "Live Now · East Coast" / "Live Now · West Coast", a Spoiler-Free Mode switch wired to the real setting (flipping it keeps the prompt open), then Stay Updated — I'm Watching Live (East: unlocks drafts, `unlock_draft_scores_through`; West: published only, `mark_episodes_watched_through`), Mark Watched — I've Finished It (grayed with no helper during East; enabled in West, unlocks drafts and catches up) and Dismiss (stays gated; remembered per week *and coast* per device, so a West prompt follows an East dismiss). Someone who chose East Stay isn't asked again in West. The coast split is enforced in the app only: Mark Watched can already unlock drafts at any time, so a server-side check would add nothing. Whenever released drafts exist that the viewer hasn't unlocked, and they have chosen neither Stay Updated nor Mark Watched, a Spoiler-Free-agnostic soft-inset strip ("Draft scores available", "Latest: {First} & {First} · N/M" — released couples over couples dancing that week, `draftReleaseLabel`, pill **Stay Updated** unlocks drafts except during the West window, when it is published-only) replaces the posting strip. A viewer who chose West Stay Updated (published only) while drafts are out sees Watching live · Week N. That choice does not raise `draft_unlocked_week`, so the amber strip stays hidden. Every strip Stay Updated pill opens the same **Scores Are Going Live** sheet as the automatic prompt (`LiveScoresSheet` / `LiveScoresPill` in `live-scores-prompt.tsx`), for the week's current coast, with no chip outside both live windows; Stay Updated inside it unlocks directly (no separate confirm step). The strip's Mark Watched (ready strip) keeps its own Mark Week N Watched sheet. **Stay Updated is the only opt-in label** on every surface (prompt, posting strip pill, draft strip pill); "Follow along…" survives only as the prompt's helper text. The posting strip's Stay Updated unlocks drafts during the East window, and follows published scores only otherwise (West included); the ready strip keeps Mark Watched. The Settings "I last watched" picker is not locked mid-East. A Spoiler-Free-off viewer who is gated gets the posting strip too (without the "Spoiler-Free ·" label); the strip's Mark Watched is the way back, and Settings has an "I last watched" picker under the Spoiler-Free toggle (the four most recent options, newest first, with None treated as week 0 so it appears only while it is still inside that window; a later current mark stays listed when it falls outside those four, so a wrong pick can still be moved; helper "Fell behind? Hide unwatched results."; its data is prefetched after paint by `useSpoilerProgress`, `src/lib/spoiler-progress.ts`, so it adds no queries to page loads and is ready when the sheet opens) that moves the mark either way: forward through `unlock_draft_scores_through` (it also unlocks released drafts, the same call East Stay Updated makes, so it is deliberately not locked mid-East), back through `unmark_episodes_watched_from` (`supabase/apply-unmark-watched.sql`). 7-day joins sit above it. Your Leagues on Home (`docs/design/home-hybrid/`, locked 2026-09-28) stacks a `LeagueTriageCard` for every league — picks-due first, no cap — with name, rank · pts, a Picks Due pill only while picks are due (a weeks-behind pill still shows when the viewer is behind; there is no caught-up pill), the Curtain Call → Dance Card → Grand Finale stack from `buildModuleStack`, and Make/Edit Picks · Open Standings · gear right to League Settings (`from` is `/` on Home; couple names on the card open the couple page, which links back there). One league hides Manage › and puts quiet Create a League / Join a League under the card (`homeLeagueChrome`). Two or more show Manage › to `/leagues` (create, join, leave, archive — housekeeping, not the only place triage lives). The full-weight "+ Create a League" / "Join with Code" buttons (same as Manage Leagues, not the quiet style) also sit under the league list in the Account Settings sheet (`LeagueSettingsLinks`), below every league row, regardless of league count — the one create/join entry point that's always reachable no matter how many leagues a viewer has. Picks due: Make Picks is primary and module lines show need states. Caught up and locked: no due pill, See Picks is a clickable outline link to that league’s Picks tab (`picksButtonLabel`), Open Standings is primary. A due card keeps the slim gold left edge. Manage Leagues uses the same card; its Week N stays once under that page title, hidden for a Spoiler-Free viewer who is behind. Stack lines carry a tone (gold = needs a pick) plus a right-aligned italic "Locked" or "Locks in …". Curtain Call lists Home and High on separate lines, each as the couple's first names (`Tatyana & Jan`, same as Dance Card); every couple name on the card (Home, High, roster, Next elim) sets only the celebrity in bold (`StackRow`); Dance Card lists each couple on its own line as first names for both partners (`Tatyana & Jan` via `danceCardRosterNames`, with the usual last-initial disambiguation), struck through once the couple is eliminated or withdrawn as far as the viewer has watched (`spoilerSafeCoupleStatus` against the same cutoff, so Spoiler-Free viewers see no strike before they mark that week; Kylie, 2026-11-04). Grand Finale shows "Next elim · {name}" from the viewer's own order via `nextPredictedElimination` over spoiler-clamped couples (`loadModuleStackInputs`, `src/lib/league-module-stack-data.ts`), so it never advances past an elimination the viewer hasn't watched. Leave League lives in League Info in League Settings (commissioners see "Commissioners can't leave"), not on Manage Leagues. Home and Manage Leagues share `loadHomeLeagueData` (`src/lib/home-league-data.ts`). There is no separate picks-deadline line or thin Make picks ticket on Home — each card's own countdown (`Locks in …`) and Make/Edit Picks button own that job.

- **Guest browsing**: sign-in is not a gateway. A signed-out visitor sees Home (curtain banner, season track, Recent Activity, and a Play Along sign-in card where Your Leagues sits) and Results (every published week), under `GuestStickyTopBar` (wordmark + Sign In) and the normal tab bar. `FanBottomNav` with no league sends Picks/Standings to `/picks` and `/standings` (`GuestTabPage`): a sign-in card for guests, a redirect to the first league's tab (or Manage Leagues) for signed-in viewers. Guests never see a live reveal, drafts or anything league-scoped. `anon` has SELECT on the season tables only (seasons, weeks, episodes, couples, people, dance styles, dance scores, episode results, In Jeopardy) plus `active_season_id`, and restrictive anon-only policies limit scores, outcomes and In Jeopardy to published episodes, since those tables' policies are otherwise `using (true)` (`supabase/apply-guest-browsing.sql`, tested in `guest-access-db.test.ts`). Everything else (Settings, Manage Leagues, league pages, the draft, admin) still requires sign-in.

- **Season wrap** (Kylie, 2026-11-24, `docs/design/season-wrap/`, Home A): once a viewer has watched the Finale (their spoiler-clamped statuses name a winner; guests once it's published), and after the Finale's West feed ends (`finaleStillAiring`, so nothing names a finisher before the West Coast has seen it), every tab switches to the season's end, except a league that never scored. Places come from points, so tied totals share a place and a crown and tied module leaders are listed together (`placeByPoints`). **Home**: the banner's season wrap (above), and each league card on Home and Manage Leagues becomes its final result (`FinalLeagueCard` in `league-triage-card.tsx`, from `LeagueHomeSummary.topStandings`): your place with a 👑 Champion pill when you won, the top three, **Final Standings** and **Season Recap** (Picks `?week=season`). **Results**: the Finale's week opens on **Final Placements** 1st–5th in place of the Eliminated banner (`finalPlacements`), then **Season at a Glance**, who went home each week before the Finale, newest first, a week with nobody out kept (`seasonEliminationOrder`). **Standings**: titled **Final Standings**, a podium for the top three managers and **Module Leaders**, who led each module on from the same stored module totals the rows show (`FinalStandings`); the full table and Score History stay. **Picks**: opens on the **Season Recap** page, the Curtain Call carousel's last page after the Finale's week (`?week=season`; Week 11's › leads there): final standing, best week (`bestWeek`, stored weekly totals), weeks your Home pick went home (`eliminationPicksCalled`, Eliminated only, as Curtain Call scores), Dance Card total and rank, and the bracket graded against the real top five (`gradedBracket`, places off, no re-score); Transactions is hidden. Pure helpers: `src/lib/season-wrap.ts`. **4th and 5th are finalists, never Eliminated, on every fan surface** (Kylie, 2026-11-24): display sites check `isPlacedFinalist` (`spoiler-safe-couple-status.ts`, on the spoiler-safe status) and read `final_place`: Results badges `4th Place` / `5th Place` (`fanOutcomeBadge(outcome, finalPlace)`) and no fading; Home's Recent Activity says `won the Mirrorball Trophy` / `finished 4th` (`addActivityOutcome`); roster tags (`clampRosterCoupleForWeek` → `fourth_place` / `fifth_place`); no strike-through on the Home card; full colour on the Couples Leaderboard; the couple page; the Grand Finale list. Scoring and eligibility keep reading the stored `eliminated` plus `final_place`; never pass a display status to `eliminationPositionRanges`.

- **Results gold lines** (`coupleLeagueNotes`, `src/lib/couple-league-notes.ts`, wired in `src/app/this-week/page.tsx`, rendered under each couple by `WeeklyResultsView`'s `leaguesByCouple`): small gold "why this couple matters to you" notes across all the viewer's leagues, in this order — "On your {leagues} roster(s)", "Your {leagues} elim pick(s)", "Your {leagues} top-scorer pick(s)", "Your {leagues} bracket next-elim pick(s)", "Your {leagues} winner pick(s)". The league phrase collapses: every one of the viewer's leagues (when they're in more than one) → "all-leagues"; 3+ but not all → "N leagues'"; 1–2 → the names ("Alpha and Beta"). The noun pluralizes when several leagues share it. Grand Finale's next-elim is `nextPredictedElimination` over couples spoiler-clamped to the *selected* week, so a bracket can't reveal an elimination the viewer hasn't reached; "bracket next-elim pick" (not "next-elim pick") keeps it from reading as Curtain Call's one-week "elim pick". Winner pick only shows in the semi-final and finale weeks (`finaleWeekNumber - 1` onward), since a mid-season predicted winner isn't a per-week stake.

- **League at a Glance** = every *other* manager's picks, appended to the bottom of the module's existing card on Picks (never a separate card; the viewer is excluded because their own picks are the card above). Curtain Call (`CurtainCallLeagueList`) and Dance Card (`DanceCardLeagueList`) follow whatever week that card's carousel shows, and order managers by points that week then alphabetically; carousels are untouched. Grand Finale (`GrandFinaleLeagueList`, inside `GrandFinaleBox`, once locked) has no week: it shows each manager's "next predicted elimination" via `nextPredictedElimination` (`src/lib/grand-finale-pins.ts`) — the couple in the next elimination slot, computed against the *viewer's* spoiler-clamped couples so no bracket can spoil a viewer who is behind. Expand/collapse state persists per viewer through `usePersistedState` (except Your Season Bracket on Picks, which deliberately reopens collapsed every visit) (`src/lib/use-persisted-state.ts`, the repo's only `localStorage` use). The Curtain Call per-pick lines are recomputed live while the week total is the stored `weekly_manager_scores` value, so they disagree until a week is republished after a scoring-setting change.

- **Score History** (Standings): each leaderboard row expands in place (`StandingsLeaderboard` → `ScoreHistoryPanel`; several can be open at once) into a fixed-height (`h-72`) panel whose feed scrolls inside, with the module chips and a collapsible totals bar scrolling with it. It shows that manager's nonzero "+X from Y" lines, newest week first, filtered by module chips (multi-select; none selected = All). There is **no stored ledger**: `getScoreHistory` (`src/app/leagues/[id]/score-history/actions.ts`) → `loadScoreHistory` (`src/lib/score-history-data.ts`, runs on the *viewer's* client so RLS gates peers' picks) → `buildScoreHistory` (`src/lib/score-history.ts`, pure) re-derives lines from the scoring engine's own helpers (couples are named by celebrity first name only, to keep lines on one row). Lines carry the category weight (`× 0.5 Wt` suffix only when ≠ 1); `score-history.test.ts` checks they sum to `computeWeeklyScores`/`computeGrandFinalePoints`. The sticky totals bar shows the stored weighted season/module totals, which stay authoritative if lines drift by rounding or a pre-publish settings change. Spoiler-free mode limits it to `resolveSpoilerCutoff` weeks. **Anchor Week gates every module**: weeks before the league's Anchor Week (`scoring_settings.judges_score_starts_week`) pay nothing — Dance Card, Curtain Call and Grand Finale alike — in both `recomputeWeekScores` (`results.ts`) and the history builder; changing it only takes effect on the next publish of each week. Grand Finale credits land in the week a couple's outcome is in `RESOLVING_OUTCOMES`; podium (placement) lines land in that same week.

- **Couple history** (`/couples/[id]`, Kylie picked a page over a sheet, 2026-11-05; mocks in `docs/design/couple-history/`): one couple's season, newest week first — each dance (`nightDanceLabel`), its song and raw total, judges' marks for signed-in viewers only (guests have no `judge_scores` grant; names drop the surname, "Carrie Ann 8"), a Night line (`collapseToNights`) only when a night has more than one row, and the week's outcome / In Jeopardy; stats are the average night on the 30 basis, Best (the couple's own dance with the largest share of its marks possible, so 30/30 beats 38/40, shown raw with its out-of; special dances and a Team Dance night's Freestyle never count; Kylie, 2026-11-05) and In Jeopardy weeks. A special dance reads as its type with its name beside it (`specialDanceTypeAndName`: **Team Dance** (Slaytopia), but **Dance-Off 1**); the dance count includes dance-off heats but not an immunity bonus. Results cards and the Eliminated banner, Couples Leaderboard rows, Your Fantasy Roster cards, Home Recent Activity, the league cards on Home and Manage Leagues, League at a Glance, the saved/locked Grand Finale brackets and Recast's available couples link to it with `coupleHistoryHref(id, from)` (`CoupleNameLink`; no chevron or underline, Kylie 2026-11-06; never inside a button, so a Grand Finale peer's Next elim line and the bracket being built stay plain); `coupleHistoryReturn` follows only a known in-app `from` (Results, Picks with its weeks, Standings `#couples`, Recast, Home, Manage Leagues) for the back link and the tab bar's league, else Results. The tab bar highlights no tab there, so the tab it came from stays a link; `ScrollToHash` brings Standings back to the leaderboard. On a live night it refreshes like Results (`RevealAutoRefresh`) and names a posting week the viewer hasn't opted into, pointing to Results for Stay Updated. `loadCoupleHistory` (`src/lib/couple-history-data.ts`) does every gate on the server for the viewer: completed weeks through `resolveSpoilerCutoff` with outcomes, plus a revealing week's posted dances (no outcome) only when `loadRevealingWeek` says it's visible; guests get published weeks. Header status goes through `spoilerSafeCoupleStatus`, counts and stats cover shown weeks only, and the "Weeks 7–8 aren't watched yet" row comes from the season (`unwatchedRange`), never the couple's own weeks, so it reads the same for a couple already eliminated; it points to Results instead of carrying a Mark Watched button (the strip's sheet, with Choose an Earlier Week, stays the one control). Released drafts are not shown (Results doesn't show them either). Under the stats, signed-in viewers get **In Your Leagues** (mock B): one row per league of theirs whose Dance Card has drafted, the holder (your roster with `YouPill`, co-managers included; another manager's; or Undrafted) and the couple's judges' points there, read off that league's own Couples Leaderboard row (`buildCoupleLeagueRows`, `src/lib/couple-leagues.ts`, over the page's own weeks; an undrafted couple's what-if from the Anchor Week). Rows are not links. On a draft night Standings also counts released drafts and this card doesn't, until the week posts. Each shown results week ends with Results' gold pick lines for that week (`coupleWeekPickNotes`: Curtain Call elim and top-scorer picks, the bracket's next elimination as of that week, the winner pick from the semi-final; each league's own team id, so co-managers see their team's picks), never a roster line (In Your Leagues names the holder once), and none on a Posting Live week. Unknown ids and other seasons' couples are `notFound()`. Pure logic: `buildCoupleHistory` (`src/lib/couple-history.ts`).
