# Lifeline, Recast and Trades

The three optional roster mechanisms, the Transactions card and pages they share, and what happens when a manager leaves after the draft.

Dated notes such as "(Kylie, 2026-11-10)" record when that behaviour was settled.

- **Lifeline and the Equalizer (Kylie, 2026-11-10, `docs/design/lifeline-recast-trades/plan.md`; `supabase/apply-lifeline.sql`, applied live 2026-11-11 and verified):** a commissioner opt-in in League Settings → Dance Card (`LifelineSettings`, default off). A manager with a dead roster slot (no couple in it still dancing that week: its couple eliminated or withdrawn in an earlier week, or nobody in it because a trade emptied it, Kylie 2026-11-19, `supabase/apply-lifeline-open-spots.sql`; `lifeline_slot_is_dead`, mirrored by `isDeadSlot` and `lifelinePicksState`; the Equalizer still counts only spots a couple held) picks one couple still dancing that they don't hold, per slot per week, on `/leagues/[id]/lifeline` (`LifelineCard`, opened from Picks; `loadLifelineWeek`, `src/lib/lifeline-week-data.ts`, shared with Picks; `saveLifelinePicks` → `set_lifeline_pick` / `clear_lifeline_pick`; locks at `lifeline_lock_at`; peers' picks are hidden until the lock; a viewer who is behind gets a catch-up state, `lifelinePicksState`, and the page shows any viewer who is behind only the catch-up card). The pick pays `max(0, 0.5 × that couple's Dance Card credit that week)` whatever the couple's outcome (Kylie, 2026-11-10: a couple that goes home still pays, its credit just has no survival step; a bye has no credit, so it pays 0), then the Dance Card weight (`buildLifelinePayouts`, `src/lib/lifeline.ts`). **`weekly_manager_scores` is unchanged**: its writers delete and reinsert rows, so Lifeline lives in `lifeline_picks` / `lifeline_payouts` / `league_point_adjustments`, and **every reader that shows or ranks totals reads the `weekly_manager_standings` view** (one row for every team and week with a score row, a payout or an adjustment, so a payee with no score row still counts; `supabase/apply-lifeline-standings-payees.sql`, Codex 2026-11-20) through `loadWeeklyStandings` (`src/lib/weekly-standings.ts`: the stored row plus `lifeline_points`, `transfer_points` and a `total_points` that includes both; it falls back to the table while the view is missing). Publication settles Lifeline in the same transaction: the plan carries `lifelinePayouts` and `lifelineEqualizers`, and `apply_lifeline_settlement` checks their coverage and refuses a plan without them while a league plays. The **Equalizer** is a one-time catch-up for slot-weeks already lost (0.5 × the mean credit of the couples that danced, one that went home that week included, the same as a pick on it would pay; per dead slot-week from the Anchor Week to the week before Lifeline starts): turning Lifeline on opens a confirm sheet with every manager's amount and the standings before and after (`loadLifelinePreview`, a dry run), the commissioner confirms with or without it (`confirmLifeline` → service-only `enable_lifeline`), and it is stored as one `lifeline_equalizer` adjustment on the latest scored week, recomputed on a re-publish. Score History adds `Lifeline: {couple}` and `Lifeline Equalizer` lines (Kylie, 2026-11-11: the name League Settings uses, not "Backfill") under Dance Card (`withLifelineLines`); Standings' Dance Card total includes them (`storedDanceCardPointsByManager`); the Couples Leaderboard does not. A league can't switch Dance Card version once it has Lifeline points (trigger).

- **Recast v2 (Mirrorball's waiver wire; `supabase/apply-recast-v2.sql`, rehearsed in `recast-v2-db.test.ts`, applied live 2026-11-11 and verified, the `pg_cron` job scheduled):** League Settings → Recast is Off or On (Waiver Wire); on always means reverse Dance Card standings, with no commissioner step. On `/leagues/[id]/waivers` (`RecastPanel`) a manager keeps a ranked, private list (`saveRecastList` → `save_recast_list`, which replaces the list): each claim fills an open spot (a couple who went home, or a spot a trade emptied, shown as Open Spot) or is paired with a drop of a couple still dancing, and the drop only happens if that claim wins. `process_due_recasts` (service only, called by `pg_cron` every 15 minutes, `supabase/queries/schedule-recast-cron.sql`) decides each league once per completed week, 48 hours after the week's results are posted (Kylie, 2026-11-10, `supabase/apply-recast-after-results.sql`, applied live 2026-11-11 and verified; the last night of a multi-night week counts, and until every night is posted `recast_next_cycle.due_at` is null and the app says "48 hours after Week N results are posted"; it never runs without results); `recast_cycles` stamps the run, so it is idempotent. Managers take turns in reverse Dance Card standings (Kylie, 2026-11-13, `supabase/apply-recast-dance-card-order.sql`: a claim only changes Dance Card points, so the wire goes first to the roster that needs it; `recast_order`, mirrored by `recastOrder` in `src/lib/recast.ts`: fewest Dance Card points as Standings shows them, raw `roster_points` × the league's Dance Card weight plus the already-weighted `lifeline_points` and `transfer_points`, then the lower latest week, then the later draft pick; the Waiver Order fold shows that Dance Card figure, not the leaderboard total), each awarded at most one claim a round, their highest that can still win; every award sends the turn back to the top of the order, so a manager waiting on a couple someone else is dropping takes it ahead of anyone after them (`run_recast_claims`). A winner starts scoring in the next week that hasn't aired (`recast_next_cycle` gives the due time and start week); the dropped couple shows on the wire as a pending drop and can be claimed in the same run; lists clear after each run, a manager can't reclaim a couple they dropped in that run or the next, and there is no run after the Finale. A week that came due before Recast was turned on (`leagues.recast_on_since`) never gets a run, and `save_recast_list` refuses claims for one that Trades is keeping alive. The file is safe to apply again: saved lists are renumbered only on the first apply. Missed claims are shown only to their owner (`RECAST_MISS_COPY`); awards are public, spoiler-gated, in the page's Adds and Drops log and on Home's Recent Activity. A site admin sees `RecastOverdueNotice` on Enter Results when a run is due and hasn't happened, which now only means the job is stuck (`recast_runs_overdue`). The first-come, manual and commissioner-processed RPCs are dropped (no league had Recast on when the SQL went in). In-app copy says "waiver". Rules copy is `RECAST_RULES`, shown on the page and in League Settings.

- **Trades (`supabase/apply-trades.sql`, rehearsed in `trades-db.test.ts`, applied live 2026-11-11 and verified):** its own switch in League Settings → Dance Card (`TradesSettings`, `set_trades_enabled`, default off, independent of Recast; turning it off cancels open offers and accepted trades). On `/leagues/[id]/trades` (`TradesPanel`) a trade is up to 12 legs, each moving one couple or an amount of points from one manager to another, so it can be uneven or involve more than two managers; it has to move at least one couple (`tradeDraftProblem`, `src/lib/trades.ts`; `propose_trade` is the authority). An offer is private to its managers until every one accepts (`respond_to_trade`; any of them can decline, the proposer can cancel); an accepted trade can't be called off, is posted to the league for 24 hours, and is vetoed if more than half of the teams not in it vote to (`veto_trade`, `vetoVotesNeeded`; none possible when every team is in it; only votes from teams still in the league count). **It takes effect when its veto window closes** (Kylie, 2026-11-12, `docs/design/trade-execution/plan.md`; `supabase/apply-trade-execution.sql`, rehearsed in `trades-db.test.ts`, applied live 2026-11-12 by Kylie): `process_due_trades` (service only, called at the end of `process_due_recasts` by the same `pg_cron` tick, after the due waiver runs so a run's order is read before any trade's points move) runs `run_league_trades` for every league with Trades on, with the start week `release_effective_week()` (the first week whose first night hasn't aired, a removal's rule; `week_number` is the week before it). `run_league_trades` holds while any aired competition night is unpublished, since `couples.status` only changes at Finish Week, and fails a trade whose start week is past the Finale with `no_week_left`; `process_league_recast` still calls it first, so a trade clearing at a run goes before the claims. The app must not be deployed before the SQL: the page would promise "Takes effect then" while the live functions still wait for a run. Couples change rosters from the start week, and points land as two `trade_transfer` rows in `league_point_adjustments` on the latest scored week (`transfer_points` in the view). `tradeEffectiveWeek` (`src/lib/trades.ts`) mirrors the week for the page (read a `TRADE_TICK_MS` after the window, naming the results it waits on); the facts strip says `Couples move · Week 10` for a trade accepted now. The veto is visible to everyone outside the trade: `RosterMoves.trades.vetoes` gives the Trades tab its needs-you dot until the viewer has voted, the Home card a gold `Trade up for veto · Ends in 20h`, and the Transactions card `Kim and Pat's trade is up for veto · Ends tomorrow 5 PM PT` (plain `…, up for veto · You voted` after); the viewer's own accepted trade reads `Trade with Kim, accepted · Clears tomorrow 5 PM PT` (`Clears in 20h` on Home). Across all trades a manager can send at most 10% of the points they have earned, transfers excluded (`trade_cash_available`). A trade goes through whole or not at all (`TRADE_FAILURE_COPY`); a couple in an open trade can't be in a waiver claim and the reverse, first submitted wins. An offer lapses 48 hours after it was sent (`trade_offers.expires_at`) or at `trade_deadline` (the Semi-Finals lock), whichever is first (`offerExpiresAt`; each open offer shows the time). The next waiver run marks it `expired`; until then it is still stored as `proposed`, so `respond_to_trade` refuses to accept it, the claim guard stops holding its couples and the page reads it as Expired. A viewer who is behind sees a catch-up card and no offers or rosters; the Trade Log holds back what they haven't watched (`tradeLogVisible`). Score History shows `Trade: points to / from {manager}` under Dance Card (`withTradeLines`), counted in the Dance Card total. Rules copy is `TRADE_RULES`.

- **Lifeline, Recast and Trades open from Picks, not the top bar** (Kylie, 2026-11-10, `docs/design/picks-roster-moves/`; Lifeline joined the row that night, its card leaving Picks for its own page): `RosterMoves` (`src/components/roster-moves.tsx`) is its own card titled Transactions (Kylie, 2026-11-11: the fantasy-sports word; the identifiers keep the old roster-moves name) under Your Fantasy Roster (Kylie, 2026-11-10, `docs/design/roster-moves-card/` A: its lines are about the weeks to come, so they don't sit in a card paged to an earlier week; the title has no week, each line names its own), once the draft is complete; a manager with no couple in the paged week has no roster card, so the card follows the draft status card. Standings has no entry point, and `TopBar` is only the wordmark and avatar. **The card and the three pages share one switch** (Kylie, 2026-11-11, `docs/design/roster-moves-pages/`, card C1 over the pages "as a whole": the same three things were a vertical list on Picks and a horizontal switch on the pages, which read as two different sets): `RosterMovesSwitch` (`src/components/roster-moves-switch.tsx`), Lifeline | Recast | Trades in that order, only the mechanisms the league has on, a gold dot (plus sr-only "needs you") on each that still needs the viewer, and never rendered with one tab (one segment reads as a broken switch). The tabs come from `rosterMoveTabs` / `defaultRosterMoveTab` (`src/lib/league-triage.ts`, tested): needs-you is a missing Lifeline before its lock (`lifelinePickDue`), an open spot with no claim saved, an offer waiting on the viewer, or a trade up for veto the viewer hasn't voted on. Every surface reads the moves through `loadRosterMoveTabs` (`src/lib/roster-moves-data.ts`) → `loadHomeRosterMoves`, the Home card's loader, so Home, Picks and the page dots can't disagree; `RosterMoves<Couple>` carries each Lifeline spot's former couple and pick, each claim's add and drop, and each offer's sender (`mapRosterMoveCouples` renames ids). On the card the switch is real tabs (client state, opens on the first that needs you) over the selected move's lines and one gold `Open Recast ›` link (`rosterMoveTabHref`, carrying the Picks weeks as `from`); a viewer who is behind gets no card, only `LifelineCatchUpCard` / `RecastCatchUpCard`. Lifeline lines: `Harry & Witney, in place of Alix & Val` per spot (a gold "No pick" when one is missing, until the lock) or a gold "1 open spot · No Lifeline saved" (Lifeline, Recast and Trades all call it an open spot), then `Saved · Week 10 locks today 8 PM PT.` (`1 of 2 saved`, `None saved · Week 10 locked.`), "Every couple on your roster is still dancing, so there's no Lifeline to pick in Week 10." with no dead spot (the loader sends no spots); a spot a trade emptied reads `in place of` the couple that last held it (`, for an open spot` if none ever did); "Nothing to pick this week." when there is no Lifeline week. Recast lines: each claim in rank order (`1 · Add Jo & Ezra, open spot` / `…, drop Alix & Val`) or a gold "1 open spot · No waiver claim saved", then `2 saved, waiting on the run · Thursday 5 PM PT.` (`Next run Thursday 5 PM PT.`, "None saved · …", "48 hours after Week 9 results are posted" before the time is known) plus `Winning claims start scoring in Week 10.` (only with a claim saved or a couple free to claim); "No waiver run to come." after the last run or when the list couldn't be read. Trades lines: a gold `Offer from Kim is waiting on you · Ends Friday 5 PM PT` per offer, `Trade with Kim, accepted · Clears tomorrow 5 PM PT` per accepted trade, `Kim and Pat's trade is up for veto · Ends …` per open window the viewer is outside, else "No offer waiting on you." Times on the card put Today/Tomorrow in lower case (`inSentence`). Small icons mark what is settled (Kylie, 2026-11-10, `docs/design/roster-moves-icons/`, Recast 2): a green check on a saved Lifeline, a dashed gold circle on an empty spot, missing pick or waiting offer, and an hourglass on each waiver claim and accepted trade, since neither is settled until the run; the couple coming in is in the foreground colour. **The pages** (`/leagues/[id]/lifeline`, `/waivers`, `/trades`) share `RosterMovesFrame` (`src/components/roster-moves-frame.tsx`): a `‹ Picks` back link that says where it goes (`RosterMovesBackLink`, 44px tall on touch; `rosterMovesBackLabel`: Picks, Home, Manage Leagues, else Back; couple links on all three pages carry the page and its own `from`, so the way back from a couple keeps the Picks weeks), the title Transactions, the same switch as links on this page's tab, a two-fact strip (times through `useFormattedDeadline`: Lifeline `Week 10 locks` / `Pays · Half their Dance Card`; Recast `Next run` / `Claims start`; Trades `Trades close` / `Couples move`), the viewer's own to-do first, and the reference folded under it in `FoldCard`s (closed on every visit, not persisted): League Lifelines (with "Shown once Week 10 locks") and How Lifeline Works (`LIFELINE_RULES`); Waiver Order (noting "You're 3rd of 6"), Adds and Drops and How Recast Works; Trade Log and How Trades Work. A page whose mechanism is off or whose draft isn't done shows `RosterMovesUnavailable` with the same back link. Recast: a claim is made from The Wire (Claim on each couple opens a bottom sheet of the viewer's spots, Open Spot or `Drop Alix & Val`, one tap saves through `saveRecastList`; a couple already listed shows `On your list · 2`, which reopens the sheet for a fallback claim on another spot), the list keeps its ↑ ↓ ✕ rows, and the old Add / In Place Of selects are gone. Trades: a two-manager trade the viewer is in reads as You give / You get boxes (`tradeSides`; anything else lists its parts), an offer waiting on the viewer is a gold-ringed card, and Propose a Trade is a With select over the same two boxes, each with `+ Add a Couple or Points` opening a sheet of that side's couples and a points field; `+ Add Another Manager` opens the From / To / Sends form that builds any trade one part at a time (both emit `TradeLeg[]` through `tradeDraftProblem` → `proposeTrade`). Lifeline: one block per empty spot with a gold "No pick yet" under an empty select; saving with every spot empty keeps the form open. **The Home league card carries the same moves inside its Dance Card row, under the couples** (Kylie, 2026-11-11, `docs/design/home-card-roster-moves/`: the lines she picked, kept under Dance Card, with no row or label of their own; Manage Leagues uses the same card): a gold dashed circle on `Need Lifeline pick`, `1 open spot` and `1 trade offer`, a green check on `Lifeline: Maura & Mark`, an hourglass on `Add Tatyana & Jan, +1 more` (the top claim only) and `Trade with Kim` (an accepted trade waiting on its veto window), a gold dashed circle on `Trade up for veto`, each mechanism's clock on its first line in its largest unit only (`Locks in 5h`, `Runs in 2d` / `After results` / `Runs soon`, `Ends in 20h`, `Clears in 20h`), a muted `No Lifeline pick` once the lock passed. Pure rows: `rosterMoveRows` in `src/lib/league-triage.ts` (`ModuleLine.moves`, none before the draft is complete); reads: `loadHomeRosterMoves` (`src/lib/home-roster-moves-data.ts`, inside `computeLeagueHomeSummary`'s second query round, only the mechanisms the league has on, nothing for a viewer who is behind). **A missing Lifeline before its lock counts as Picks Due** (`lifelinePickDue`, in `summary.picksDue`, so Home, Manage Leagues and the league switcher agree); an open spot, a trade offer or a trade up for veto is gold but not Picks Due. An open spot only shows (and only dots the Recast tab) while the wire has a couple to claim, one still dancing on no roster or offered as another manager's drop (`openSpotsToFill`, `RosterMoves.waivers.wireCount`, Kylie 2026-11-17); with none, Home says nothing and the Transactions card reads a muted `1 open spot · No couple free to claim yet`. An offer the viewer already accepted and is waiting on others for is not listed.

- **Removing a manager after the draft releases their roster** (Kylie, 2026-11-12, `docs/design/remove-manager/plan.md`; `supabase/apply-remove-manager.sql`, rehearsed in `remove-manager-db.test.ts`, applied live 2026-11-12 and read back): `remove_league_member` (commissioner) and `leave_league` (self) share `release_team`, under the `leagues` row lock the waiver run takes. The effective week is `release_effective_week()` (the first week whose first night hasn't aired, else the week after the last, so a removal between the Finale's nights keeps the Finale; `releaseEffectiveWeek` in `src/lib/remove-manager.ts` mirrors it for the dialogs). Weeks that have aired are wholly theirs and still score; every `roster_slots` row reaching into the effective week ends the week before it (rows starting at or after it, a claim won for a week not yet aired, are deleted, as is any closed row reaching past the boundary, so two managers never score one couple in a week). Their pending claims, Lifeline picks and Curtain Call picks for weeks from the effective week on, draft queue and open late unlock go; any trade offer they are in is cancelled (`proposed`) or failed with `left_league` (`accepted`) now, and accepted trades still in their veto window are re-checked against the smaller league. Score rows, payouts, the Equalizer and the Grand Finale bracket stay (a correction republish rebuilds a week's bracket points from it). `roster_releases` logs each freed couple with the manager's name at release time (the Adds and Drops fold reads `Released`, under the week before; Home's Recent Activity says `Kim left, releasing Harry & Witney and Alix & Val in Alpha`; the Couples Leaderboard shows a departed holder as **Released**). With Recast on the couples are free on the wire at once and a claim scores from the next run's start week; with Recast off nobody can take them until Recast is turned on. `league_departures` (service only) records who left a drafted league, and `join_league`, under the league lock, refuses them while the draft stays complete. Deferred constraint triggers on `lifeline_picks`, `waiver_claims` and `trade_parties` take the league row for share and refuse a row for a team that is gone at commit. Every delete is scoped to the active season, since week numbers repeat across seasons. `reset_draft` clears the log. The Remove and Leave dialogs say all of it (`removalNote`); before the draft they only drop the row, as before.
