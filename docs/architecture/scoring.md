# Scoring

Point values, weights, how judges' scores become points, and League Settings' scoring section.

Dated notes such as "(Kylie, 2026-11-10)" record when that behaviour was settled.

- **Scoring**: `scoring_settings` holds commissioner-editable point values. Defaults are strong-play season ceilings from `src/lib/strong-play-ceilings.ts` (printed by `scripts/monte-carlo-calibration/run.mjs`). A category weight is a share of that ceiling: weight 1 means Dance Card (median snake seat, skill-expected survival, season-average judges — not a perfect-30 max), Curtain Call (perfect picks), and Grand Finale (perfect bracket) each top out at the same season total. All three modules are on by default (the create dialog pre-ticks them; `create_league` only drops Grand Finale when the season has no premiere time). Grand Finale is not capped at 3/5. `create_league` seeds weight 1 for every module it turns on and does not redistribute. League Settings splits weight evenly across the modules that stay on when one is toggled (`redistributeModuleWeights` in `src/lib/scoring-neutral.ts`) and leaves point values alone. Reset to Neutral restores the strong-play point defaults plus that even split; both stop once the Season Clock lock has passed. **Judges points pay on `dance_scores.scaled_score`** (judges' average × 3, `scaledJudgesScore` in `src/lib/scoring.ts`, written beside `total_score` on every save, publish and reveal), so a guest-judge or missing-judge night stays on the three-judge basis the defaults assume; `total_score` stays the raw sum fans see, and Curtain Call's top scorer (and its within-1/within-2 bands) compares that raw total, combined across the couple's dances. **Dance Card pays a couple once per night, never once per dance**: `collapseToNights` / `nightScaledScoresByCouple` (`src/lib/scoring.ts`) turn a couple's rows for one episode into one night — all marks received over all marks possible (judges × that dance's per-judge max) × 30, plus any bonus-round points in the numerator only (`max_score = 0`), unrounded until the multiplier — so a 28/40 plus a team 36/40 is 64/80 → 24, the same as one 24/30, not 21 + 27, and a +3 dance-off on a 30/30 night is 33. A one-dance night with `maxScore` null returns the stored `scaled_score` untouched; two episodes in one week each pay their own night. `DanceScore` requires `episodeId` for that reason and carries `maxScore` (null = legacy, recovered through `judgeCountForDance`; 0 = points). Every reader that turns dance rows into judges points (engine, publish/reveal/recompute rows, Couples Leaderboard, Score History, roster lines on Picks/Standings, draft-night standings, the live/draft merges keyed by episode + couple) goes through those helpers. Score History shows both on such a night (`Jdg 38→28.5`, `Jdg 64→24`); Results cards keep scores off the dance lines: a card with more than one dance shows each dance's score in small type under the card total on the right (`28 + 36`, a bonus round in gold as `+3 bonus`), with no "out of" (Kylie, 2026-11-04; `coupleDanceLines`, `src/lib/night-dance-lines.ts`; a Freestyle on a Team Dance episode reads `Freestyle (Team)`, not for guests, who can't read round types); the Enter Results Leaderboard header reads `64/80 → 24.00`. `supabase/apply-strong-play-ceilings.sql` predates this rule and is historical — never rerun it. `judges_score_multiplier` is the roster-size lever (`dance_card_calibration`), guarded by `judges_score_multiplier_customized` so a draft start never clobbers a commissioner override. Saving that roster-size calibration clears the flag (`supabase/apply-reset-scoring-neutral.sql`) so Reset to Neutral lets a later draft start seed again. It is not part of the Season Clock lock: it stays editable before and during the draft, including after the Grand Finale deadline, so a late `start_draft` can still write the calibration. Once `leagues.draft_status = completed` it locks — `update_scoring_categories` rejects a different value (`supabase/apply-lock-judges-score-multiplier.sql`) and League Settings disables the field. `reset_draft` returns the league to `not_started`, which lifts that lock. Curtain Call payouts scale by `couplesRemaining / totalCouples` (`curtainCallPayout` in `src/lib/scoring.ts`); the picking UI renders its preview through the same helper so it can't drift from what's actually scored. Grand Finale's three methods (`distance_based`, `exact_position`, `band_tier`) share that perfect-picks ceiling — exact, distance, and equal bands use one points-per-correct; graded bands use a higher base because lower bands pay a fraction (`bandOf` / `bandPayoutFraction`, `src/lib/grand-finale-explainer.ts`). **Point scale**: the solve times `POINT_SCALE = 0.1`, so one share is 100 season points rather than 1000. Re-running the script for a future re-fit already bakes this in; don't re-derive it by hand. **Point display/rounding**: fantasy points are rounded to two decimals when computed (`roundPoints`) and always shown with two decimals (`formatPoints` / `formatSignedPoints`, `src/lib/format-points.ts`), even whole numbers (`12.00`). Raw judge scores are not fantasy points and stay plain integers. Never interpolate a points value into copy unformatted.

- **League Settings scoring** is one section in `LeagueModulesForm`: Modules (the on/off helper stays), then one collapsed section per module that is on. Turning a module on or off splits category weight evenly across the modules that stay on (a turned-off module goes to 0) and leaves point values alone. **Reset to Neutral**, on the Modules card, restores the calibrated strong-play point defaults and that even split for the modules that are on. Both are disabled once the Season Clock lock has passed. Creating a league still seeds every enabled module at weight 1; the even split starts when a module is toggled in Settings. That section opens straight onto the module's weight field — there is no Scoring Mix accordion — and a single Scoring Details expand under the weight holds that module's fields. Dance Card's Judges' Score Multiplier stays editable until the draft is complete, then the field is disabled with a locked note, and Reset to Neutral leaves it alone. Turning a module off removes its section. Season Clock (Anchor Week, Currently Locks, Update Anchor) is its own section after the modules. Grand Finale's Missed the lock / Allow late sheet lives in the Grand Finale section once the deadline has passed (commissioner or super admin, including a super admin who isn't in the league); members get the same layout read-only, without Allow late. There is no separate "Scoring by module" summary.
